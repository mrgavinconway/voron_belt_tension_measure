Set up your measurement

For checks at other gantry positions, measure the actual free belt span between supports and enter it above. Pluck near its midpoint. Front, middle and rear positions do not have a universal span.

About the targets

Voron recommends starting at 110 Hz for A/B and 140 Hz for Z, both over 150 mm. These targets apply to both printer sizes. Other spans use an approximate equivalent: reference × 150 ÷ span. Modified belts may need a different reference.

The ±5% “near target” band is an app aid, not an official Voron tolerance. A strong signal is not proof of a correct fundamental: if readings jump by roughly 2×, re-pluck and check the setup.
